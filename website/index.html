Research focus

Reliable inference when the signals are adaptive, multimodal and private.

My work asks a practical question: what can a system reliably infer about user experience when content is adaptive, signals are multimodal and network payloads are encrypted?

I combine video, network, behavioral and feedback signals, then evaluate them for generalization, calibration, privacy and deployment cost. Public-sector IT work keeps the research grounded: systems must be auditable, secure, maintainable and useful beyond a notebook.
