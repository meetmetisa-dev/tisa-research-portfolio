Research identity

From encrypted streaming telemetry to trustworthy adaptive systems.

My research asks a consistent question: how can an intelligent system recognize when its outputs remain trustworthy?

I was awarded the Ph.D. in Computer Science by United Arab Emirates University on 10 July 2026. My dissertation, Data-Driven QoE Inference for HTTP Adaptive Streaming, developed methods that integrate network measurements, playback behavior, content characteristics, facial expressions, engagement signals, and subjective feedback.

I extend this work through explainability, concept-drift monitoring, reinforcement-learning-based recovery, federated optimization, cooperative control, and expert-auditable AI systems.
