RESEARCH ARC

From encrypted packets to explainable, self-healing QoE

01

Encrypted streaming inference

Machine-learning inference of startup delay, stalls and selected bitrate from network/transport-layer signals in HTTPS and QUIC traffic. Random Forest with feature ranking reached 91.13% average classification accuracy for HTTPS and 87.34% for QUIC.

HAS → telemetry → feature ranking → Random Forest → QoE factors
02

Deep QoE inference

DSOM and multilayer perceptron backpropagation were evaluated across public YouTube datasets, extending QoE inference toward high-dimensional clustering and classification.

traffic features → DSOM / MLPB → clustering + classification → QoE
03

Emotion-aware QoE

A 2024 FER-based framework fused facial expressions, advertisement information, user feedback and ITU-T P.1203. The study reports up to 37.1% accuracy improvement over the benchmark and collected responses from 661 participants across 114 countries.

network + video + ads + facial emotion + MOS → multimodal QoE
04

AELIX: engagement + explainability

The 2025 IEEE Access work introduced Adaptive Ensemble for Imbalance Classification using eXplainable AI, integrating facial emotion, network conditions, video metadata, sentiment, SMOTEENN and SHAP. Reported accuracies reached 0.95 and 0.994 and surpassed ITU-T P.1203 by 55.3%.

multimodal signals → SMOTEENN → adaptive ensemble → SHAP → engagement-aware QoE
05

QoE-Foresight

A current manuscript proposes a closed loop: EMMA multimodal fusion, Ridge prediction, HDDE residual drift detection, DDQN self-healing and SHAP explanations. On its controlled 813-sample fused benchmark it reports R²≈0.953, HDDE F1=0.967, 35 ms detection delay and 91% recovery success.

EMMA → Ridge → residuals → HDDE → DDQN healing ↔ SHAP

Manuscript / research in progress — not presented as a published IEEE article.
